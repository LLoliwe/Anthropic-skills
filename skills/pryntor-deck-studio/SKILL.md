## ZOWIBO guardrail
The user's supplied ZOWIBO Big Little Adventures 3D artwork (the expressive children, central Black child in yellow, fantastical treehouse and floating world) remains the strongest visual contender. Preserve its characters, imagery, playful spirit and typography as visual direction. Wonderbay, OOPS! LAB and BOX NINE were rejected for flagship consideration. Do not silently replace the brand or imply an IP clearance/finished video.

## Creative and engineering sequence
1. Establish audience, intended decision, presenter versus reading deck, output type and all source/rights constraints.
2. Build a compelling story spine. Every slide has a dramatic or argumentative purpose, one decisive line, and one precise visual proof; reject generic bullet lists.
3. Produce three actually different visual explorations using Frontend Slides, use Knowledge Cat for story design/QA, use Baoyu only for rights-cleared image-first experimentation. Use Figma principles as a linked reference with authorization.
4. Create a design system (type hierarchy, palette, grid, image grammar, 16:9 crop, meaningful whitespace and deliberate rhythm). Use editable shapes/text for native presentations, retaining supplied hero art as licensed placed media.
5. Render every single slide; inspect both wall-screen and phone scales. Check line wraps, contrast, spacing, meaning-bearing art cropping, source citations, factual correctness, image consistency and native editability. An overflow detector does not replace creative evaluation.
6. Human art direction/rights/safeguarding review is mandatory before any public kids-IP pitch or release. No background spending, public uploads or unsanctioned paid generators.
