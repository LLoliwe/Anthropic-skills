2. Record **actual** Pika account balance and model-specific generation credit estimate *before* generating. If a price is in USD, requires card, or risks overage, **STOP**. If balance insufficient, STOP; do not buy credits to chase bonus.

4. After successful provider generation, independently inspect transaction/credit history for **actual grant** of 250 credits. A screenshot/banner or marketing promise alone = **unclaimed**. Note expiry/eligibility from the connected account rather than guessing.
5. Record provider asset ID, source prompt hash, selected model, timestamp, pre-balance, cost estimate, spend receipt, post-balance, bonus credit receipt, and rights terms in `references/pika-claim-receipt.example.json`-shaped record.
