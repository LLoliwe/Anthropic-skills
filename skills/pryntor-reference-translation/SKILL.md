## Mandatory transfer recipe
1. Anchor in the specific venture's **real user task**, target audience, brand master and actual editable codebase. Retain distinct venture identities. No universal Asko palette, character or product skin.
2. Observe 2–5 attributable references: creator, stable URL, rights, the *mechanism that works*, and what must never be copied. Separate facts, hypotheses and user taste. Do not ingest unlicensed proprietary pixels or scrape galleries.
3. Translate the mechanism into an **original system**, with a difference that can be stated: human warmth, credible in-context help, readable editorial pacing, tactile physics, meaningful microinteraction or accessible progressive disclosure. Document how it serves that venture rather than reproducing the reference.
4. Make at least one beautiful element **do something real**: an original illustration launches context-aware help, a demo runs actual product code, feedback responds to genuine state. No inert decorative CTA and no claim of LLM/AI when it uses deterministic rules.
5. Preserve rollback: build R1 separately from working R0. Never destroy approved Asko V2 frozen reference or overwrite LVL7 R0 while experimenting.
6. Independently test desktop 1440, tablet 768, phones 390/360, keyboard, focus, contrast, reduced-motion, actual errors/network/privacy, visual screenshots and original asset provenance. The manifest gate at tools/reference-translation/validate.mjs checks source-to-original traceability, NOT subjective beauty or legal clearance.
7. Close human gates: named creative reviewer and actual user study, independent accessibility/security, educator for educational products, IP/rights, founder production approval and independently confirmed live host. CI success is **engineering**, not commercial release.
