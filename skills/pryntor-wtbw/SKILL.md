# WTB&W | Who's the Best and Why?

Mandatory competitive-quality reasoning for meaningful Pryntor product, film, UX, game, research, coding, agent, robotics, commercialization, publishing and partnership work. It is an **operating skill, not a new agent**, not production deployment, not a tool permission. R0 unapproved spend.

**Law:** Find a proven leader for the *precise user job*, establish why their work actually succeeds, translate lawful mechanisms into a distinct Pryntor solution, fairly compare with a frozen standard, independently inspect the actual result, iterate on the same canonical asset and preserve evidence. Better than the previous Pryntor version alone is not evidence of world-class quality.

## Mandatory eight-stage loop
1. **Who are we serving?** Record canonical Linear issue, owner, user outcome, observed failure, original repository commit and constraints. A prototype, slide or unit test is not the requested final artifact.
2. **Who's best, at what?** Source at least two direct leaders and one lateral cross-industry analogue globally. Verify sources, dates, environment, actual work and legal usability. Rank by observed fit, not reputation, stars, popularity or hype. Distinguish subject-specific winners. A ranking that was not measured is a research hypothesis.
3. **Why?** Extract functional mechanics, original production craft, test methods, experience, economics and weaknesses. Cite verifiable case studies, actual demos, code or independent reviews. Document contrary evidence.
4. **What can we replicate legally?** Translate high-level principles, never a competitor's protected designs, trademarks, character/scene, code, prompt, proprietary data, voice, brand or other expression. Credit contributions and document "do not copy" boundaries. Protect Pryntor confidential differentiation, research holdouts and sources.
5. **What's our fair fight?** Preregister frozen held-out scenarios, comparators, input conditions, target devices, time/cost budget, scoring weights, required win margin, disqualifiers and independent evaluator before optimising. A peer whose product cannot actually be tested in equivalent conditions is a visual/inspiration reference, not a scored run.
6. **Build in one controlled workcell.** Reuse PRY-309 existing Discover > Plan > Execute > independently Verify > Iterate controller *only after* approved secure commissioning. No second duplicate improvement engine. Agent is not working unless identity, owner, access scope and real run receipt prove it. All new and present agents must follow PRY-AIG-001 and PRY-GOV-001/002 plus TRIAGE-001.
7. **Prove the actual artifact.** Compare current baseline, direct comparators, candidate and specific lateral mechanisms; inspect per-dimension outcomes, negative tests, cost per accepted result and practical significance. Require real video/moving performance for films, real interaction for games and applications, real held-out outcomes for research, sandbox replay for commerce and credible specialist review for child/physical safety. Builder cannot grade their own results.
8. **Iterate, learn, and release only after separate approval.** If inferior or founder rejects work, retain source and fix failures on same project. If better on the stated rubric, the status is REVIEW_REQUIRED until independent verification, human art/expert review, PRY-AIG-001, rights and founder release acceptance. Capture source capsule, test fixture, scorecard, screenshots/moving media and failure/learning record. Never call it shipped from a passing score or green CI.

## 30-Furies challenge (30 lenses, NOT 30 automatically active agents)
1-5 user outcome, first-run usability, speed/friction, inclusivity, recovery.
6-10 originality, composition, craft/material, motion/sound/interaction, emotional resonance.
11-15 logic, robustness, performance, security, reproducibility.
16-20 reference provenance, comparability, test contamination, blind human assessment, uncertainty.
21-25 buyer value, switching cost, distribution, economics, maintenance.
26-30 rights, privacy, client-separation, privilege/agent integrity, rollback and truthful claims.
Mark every unassigned reviewer/agent NOT RUN. No impersonated judge or invented "independent team".

## Workstream hard gates
- Studios / NIGHT SHIFT / AFTERLIGHT: real original canonical character mesh, native rig, motion simulation, render/video and audio, lensing/edit/colour/title type craft, licensed assets and independent complete-cut review. A still is not animated film.
- ZOWIBO / LVL7 / SYW: genuinely playable/learnable journey, child-safe engagement, accessible UX, subject-expert/educator judgement, original IP and real device proofs. No dark-pattern child retention.
- Websites/decks/BTWW/Tirisano: source-authentic and brand-distinct visuals, typography, interaction or rendered pages/slides, accessibility and human art direction; PQS-001/PRY-67 prevails.
- Recover/BeforeSpend/agent operations: consented sandbox user journeys, tenant isolation, deterministic replay, authorised platform actions, latency/economics, no fabricated revenue, no real sending or charges.
- Robotics/Lens: simulation-only safety, real latency/constraint and specialist physical-safety review before actuators.
- Research/open source/partnerships: novelty, reproducible held-out evaluation, correct authorship and rights, independent adoption/benefit, no commercial commitments without founder approval.
