2. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files that are placed on your device (computer, smartphone, tablet) when you visit a website. They are widely used to:

  • Make websites work more efficiently
  • Remember your preferences and settings
  • Provide information to the website owners about how the site is being used

Cookies can be "persistent" (remaining on your device until deleted or expired) or "session" (deleted when you close your browser).

4. Your Cookie Choices

4.1 Browser Controls

You can control and manage cookies in several ways. Most web browsers automatically accept cookies, but you can modify your browser settings to decline cookies if you prefer.

How to manage cookies in popular browsers:

  • Google Chrome: Settings → Privacy and security → Cookies and other site data
  • Mozilla Firefox: Settings → Privacy & Security → Cookies and Site Data
  • Safari: Preferences → Privacy → Cookies and website data
  • Microsoft Edge: Settings → Privacy, search, and services → Cookies and site data

For more information about cookies and how to manage them, visit www.aboutcookies.org.

4.2 Impact of Blocking Cookies

If you choose to block or delete cookies:

  • Essential cookies: The website may not function properly. Some features (like form mode preference) will not work.
  • Analytics cookies (if enabled in future): We will not be able to improve the website based on usage patterns, but all core functionality will remain.

5. Do Not Track (DNT) Signals

Some browsers have a "Do Not Track" (DNT) feature that lets you tell websites you do not want to have your online activities tracked.

We respect DNT signals. If your browser sends a DNT signal, we will not enable any optional tracking or analytics cookies.

6. Other Tracking Technologies

6.1 Web Beacons and Pixels

We do not use web beacons or tracking pixels. We do not embed invisible images or scripts that track your behavior across websites.

6.2 Local Storage

We may use browser local storage for technical purposes, such as:

  • Storing form draft data temporarily (never transmitted unless you submit the form)
  • Remembering UI preferences (dark mode, language, etc.)

Local storage data is stored on your device only and is not transmitted to our servers unless you explicitly submit a form.

7. Cookies and POPIA Compliance

Our use of cookies complies with the Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA):

  • Security: Cookie data is stored securely and not shared with unauthorized parties
  • Control: You can manage or delete cookies at any time
