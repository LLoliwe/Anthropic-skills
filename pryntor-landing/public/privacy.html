We use your personal information for the following lawful purposes:

Providing your personal information is voluntary. However, if you do not provide the requested information marked as "required" on our forms:

  • We cannot provide you with access to our services
  • We cannot communicate with you about your requests

We do not sell your personal information. We may share your information with:

We do not share your personal information with third parties for their marketing purposes.

9. Security Measures

We implement reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your personal information from unauthorized access, loss, or misuse:

  • Encryption: Server-side encryption at rest (AES-256 standard)
  • Transmission: HTTPS/TLS encryption for all data in transit
  • Access Controls: Limited access to personal information on a need-to-know basis
  • No Third-Party Analytics: We do not use third-party analytics or tracking tools by default

10. Retention Period

We retain your personal information only for as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes for which it was collected:

  • Active Inquiries: Until resolved and 30 days thereafter
  • Client Relationships: For the duration of the relationship and 7 years thereafter (for legal and accounting purposes)
  • Marketing Consent: Until you withdraw consent
  • Legal Requirements: As required by applicable law

You may request earlier deletion, subject to our legal retention obligations.

11. Your Rights Under POPIA

You have the following rights regarding your personal information:

11.1 Right to Access

Request a copy of the personal information we hold about you.

11.2 Right to Correction

Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete information.

11.3 Right to Objection

Object to the processing of your personal information on reasonable grounds.

11.4 Right to Deletion

Request deletion of your personal information, subject to legal retention requirements.

11.5 Right to Data Portability

Request your personal information in a structured, commonly used format.

11.6 How to Exercise Your Rights

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us at lebo@pryntor.co.za. We will respond to your request within a reasonable timeframe (typically within 30 days).

12. Direct Marketing

We will only send you direct marketing communications if:

  • You have explicitly consented to receive them (via the optional checkbox on our forms), OR
  • You are an existing client and the marketing relates to similar services

You may opt-out of marketing communications at any time by:

  • Clicking the "unsubscribe" link in our emails
  • Contacting us at lebo@pryntor.co.za

14. Children's Privacy
