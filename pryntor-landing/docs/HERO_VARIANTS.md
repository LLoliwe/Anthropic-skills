# Hero Headline Variants - Pryntor Landing Page

**Purpose:** Provide 3 distinct headline/subhead combinations optimized for different buyer personas, each designed to trigger immediate recognition and desire without hype or unverifiable claims.

**Design Principle:** Each variant uses the "Before/After" contrast formula to create concrete mental simulation and risk removal.

---

## Variant 1: Technical Operator (CTO/Engineering Lead)

**Target Persona:** CTOs, VPs of Engineering, Technical Architects who need to audit AI and automation systems.

### Headline
```
Governance as Operating System.
```

### Subhead
```
Before: Fragile approval chains, undocumented automations, unauditable AI decisions.
After: Codified governance, traceable AI workflows, risk-tiered controls.
```

### Why It Works
- **Specificity:** "Undocumented automations" and "traceable AI workflows" are concrete technical pain points
- **Authority:** "Codified governance" signals engineering rigor, not consultant fluff
- **Desire trigger:** "Risk-tiered controls" = sleep at night, no surprises
- **No hype:** Every word is a deliverable, not a promise

### Expected Response
"Finally, someone who gets that governance isn't a PDF, it's infrastructure."

---

## Variant 2: Founder/SME Buyer (Scaling Operations)

**Target Persona:** Founders and Ops Leaders at SMEs (10-200 employees) who are drowning in fragile processes as they scale.

### Headline
```
Your processes break when you grow. Ours compound.
```

### Subhead
```
Before: Every new hire means rewriting runbooks, every campaign risks compliance drift, every handover loses tribal knowledge.
After: Self-documenting systems, audit-ready automation, governance that scales with revenue, not headcount.
```

### Why It Works
- **Empathy:** "Rewriting runbooks" and "tribal knowledge" = founder PTSD
- **Contrast:** "Scales with revenue, not headcount" = profitability unlock
- **Desire trigger:** "Self-documenting systems" = exit-readiness, no founder dependency
- **Risk removal:** "Compliance drift" acknowledged, then solved

### Expected Response
"This is exactly what breaks at Series A. I need this yesterday."

---

## Variant 3: Regulated Environment Buyer (Compliance-Heavy Sectors)

**Target Persona:** Ops leaders in financial services, healthcare, legal, government, or any regulated industry where auditability is non-negotiable.

### Headline
```
Governance-first infrastructure for regulated operations.
```

### Subhead
```
Before: Manual approval chains that slow decisions, AI tools your auditors can't verify, automation that breaks compliance.
After: Evidence-gated workflows, policy-as-code enforcement, audit trails built in, not bolted on.
```

### Why It Works
- **Authority:** "Policy-as-code enforcement" = speaks auditor language
- **Specificity:** "AI tools your auditors can't verify" = exact pain point in 2026
- **Desire trigger:** "Audit trails built in, not bolted on" = no retrofit panic
- **Risk removal:** "Evidence-gated workflows" = defensible decisions

### Expected Response
"Our auditors asked for this exact thing last quarter. How fast can we start?"

## Implementation Recommendations

### A/B Testing Strategy
1. **Default (Live):** Use **Variant 1** (Technical Operator) as baseline since it's the most versatile
2. **Split Test:** Rotate Variant 2 and Variant 3 based on UTM source or industry segment if possible
3. **Personalization (Future):** Use form "Primary Goal" dropdown to infer persona and show matching variant on follow-up pages
