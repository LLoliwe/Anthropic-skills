## Copy Guidelines for Future Updates

1. **No em dashes** (use commas, periods, or colons instead)
2. **No invented proof** (no client logos, metrics without source, testimonials, awards)
3. **Specificity over hype** ("traceable AI workflows" > "amazing automation")
4. **Before/After structure** (triggers mental simulation, increases desire)
5. **Deliverable outcomes only** (every claim must be implementable)
6. **Technical precision** ("AES-256" > "secure", "30-day retention" > "temporary storage")
7. **Governance-first tone** (restrained, precise, technical calm)
8. **[TODO] for unknowns** (never guess company registration numbers, addresses, etc.)
