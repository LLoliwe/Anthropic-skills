## Installation gate
1. Confirm actual repository, owner, write access, baseline green CI and brand tokens.
2. Pin version and verify package-specific licenses and transitive dependencies.
3. Build one original functional screen in a disposable branch; no modification to production authentication or user data.
4. Compare baseline against pilot with build, tests, keyboard, screen-reader spot checks, focus, reduced-motion, contrast, 360/390/768/1440 screenshots and bundle measurements.
5. Independent Pryntor PQS review, human approval and a recorded decision to adopt/reject.
6. Preserve Tirisano, LVL7, Studios, BTWW and kids-specific brand identities.
