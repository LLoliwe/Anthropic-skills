03 / 03

Is this the full REA engine?

No. This public preview is a client-side source quick scan. A separate internal REA MCP lab performs a deeper fixture-only validation and does not accept public customer source uploads.

Is it a security audit?

No. Review notes are static heuristics, not verified exploits, professional security approval or a substitute for human review.

Who is this for?

Founders, developers and testers who want a quick understanding of their own or authorised code before making changes.

What happens to my files?

They're read locally in browser memory. Nothing is transmitted by this app; the exported report is generated and saved by your browser.
