EDUCATOR EXPLANATION
Temperature rises, for now.

Model: 100 g pure water initially at 20°C, standard boiling temperature 100°C at constant pressure, no heat loss. This is an energy and state illustration, not computational fluid dynamics.

At 0 kJ the water is 20°C. The next 33.44 kJ raises it to 100°C before any of the liquid turns to vapour.
