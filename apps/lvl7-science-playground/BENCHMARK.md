# LVL7 competitor benchmark and reconstruction decision
18 October 2026. Existing LVL7 / PRY-254 / PR #60. Desk benchmark from official public product descriptions and research, not a hands-on paid-app comparison or an exhaustive global ranking.

## Selection
**Tinybop Explorer’s Library is the strongest primary product reference for this specific LVL7 brief.** It combines a broad science collection with distinct interactive illustrated models. Its Simple Machines page describes manipulable machines, immediate force/cause-and-effect exploration, named illustration authorship, multilingual labels and a reviewed family handbook. States of Matter describes multiple materials, temperature changes and microscopic movement. That is a better target for our desired science playground than a catalogue of sliders and worksheets. Tinybop is proprietary; we did not access or copy its app source, art, branding, handbook or assets.

- https://tinybop.com/apps
- https://tinybop.com/apps/simple-machines
- https://tinybop.com/apps/states-of-matter

**PhET is the technical reference.** Its research page describes design from learning research, think-aloud student interviews per simulation, minimal text, animation and feedback. This informs direct manipulation, understandable representations and falsifiable simulation outcomes. It does not confer PhET's validation on our models.

- https://phet.colorado.edu/en/research
- https://arxiv.org/abs/1306.6544

**Pok Pok is a secondary reference for open-ended, age-appropriate play**, with a younger preschool focus than the full LVL7 science brief. **Marble remains the connected-curriculum reference**, using its separately attributed open data only where explicitly loaded. No market-share, learning-outcome superiority, price comparison, child testing or full competitor parity claim is made.

- https://playpokpok.com/
- https://withmarble.com/

## Implemented in existing LVL7
- Original illustrated museum room navigation, while retaining subject filtering and accessible room links.
- Explore / Challenge / Understand modes. The active experiment dominates the page; instruments and explanatory reading are secondary.
- Direct add/drag particles, keyboard-equivalent particle controls, draggable molecule tray and matched schematic compositions, coefficient pads, scene switch, drag-to-aim launch, weather/predator controls and pitch/amplitude pad.
- Chemistry shapes are known schematic templates tied to exact composition. They do not claim molecular dynamics, chemical synthesis or full electronic structure.
- Pure-model and DOM regressions: 23 checks pass, including pointer lifecycle, cancelled/invalid drops, keyboard actions, frame-based launch, mode navigation, geometry/letterboxing and original model regressions.

## Evidence limits
Native-app behaviour was not independently reproduced. Public-page claims are attributed to their authors. No new browser-rendering, physical iPhone, user-learning, expert-science or independent art approval is claimed. Existing Site audience preserved. Same existing product, source pipeline, tracker and review PR; no new venture or repository.
